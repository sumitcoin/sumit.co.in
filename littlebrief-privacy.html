LittleBrief Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

About LittleBrief

LittleBrief is a reading app for children: short news briefs, stories, pictures, daily quizzes with credits and prizes, and Kids' Corner, where students can share drawings and answers. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and how you can have it deleted. LittleBrief has no ads.

Without an account

You can read news, stories, pictures and Kids' Corner without an account. Saved posts, reading size and favourite topics stay on the phone and are not sent to us.

Information we collect

  • Account: first and last name, email address, and a securely scrambled (hashed) password that we cannot read.
  • Quizzes: the answers given, to show progress and work out credits.
  • Prizes: when a prize is redeemed, the delivery name, phone number and address, so we can send it.
  • Kids' Corner: the drawing photo or written answer, and the name, class, school and city to print with it.
  • Help & feedback: the messages you send us.
  • Notifications: if notifications are allowed, a notification code from Google Firebase, so we can tell you about new posts, quizzes and updates on your prizes and entries.

We do not collect location, contacts, the advertising ID, or payment details.

Kids' Corner

Every entry is checked by the LittleBrief team before it is published. Only published entries can be seen by other readers, together with the student's name, class, school and city as typed in the entry. A parent or teacher must agree before a child sends an entry. Entries can be edited or deleted in the app until they are published; to remove a published entry, contact us.

How we use and share information

  • To run the app: accounts, quizzes, credits, prize deliveries, Kids' Corner and notifications.
  • Delivery details are seen only by the LittleBrief team and the courier who delivers the prize.
  • Notifications are sent through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

Children

LittleBrief is made for children. A parent or guardian should help create the account and fill in any delivery address. Parents can ask to see, correct or delete their child's information at any time.

Security and keeping data

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on our server. We keep account information while the account exists, and delivery details as long as needed to deliver a prize and handle any problem with it.

Deleting your account and data

Send a message from Profile → Help & feedback in the app, or email us at littlebriefapp@gmail.com, from the account's email address. We delete the account, its quiz answers, credits, Kids' Corner entries and delivery details within 30 days.

Deleting some of your LittleBrief data (keeping your account)

You can remove some information and keep using your LittleBrief account:

  • A Kids' Corner entry that isn't published yet: in the app, open Kids' Corner → My entries and tap Delete. It is removed at once, with its photo.
  • A published Kids' Corner entry, a delivery address, or your quiz history: send a message from Profile → Help & feedback, or email littlebriefapp@gmail.com from your account's email address, and say what you want deleted.
  • Things kept only on your phone (saved posts, reading size, favourite topics, hidden entries): remove them in the app, or uninstall LittleBrief.

What is deleted and what is kept: we delete what you ask for within 30 days. Deleting quiz history also removes the credits earned from it. For a prize that is still being delivered, we keep the delivery details until it arrives, then delete them. We keep your name and email for as long as your account exists, so you can sign in.
